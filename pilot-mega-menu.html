MenuPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

MenuPilot is a Shopify app provided by Arif Ansari that helps merchants create, customize, translate and publish storefront navigation menus. This policy explains how information is handled when merchants use the app and visitors view its menus.

Information we process

  • Store and account information: Shopify store domain, installation status, authentication tokens, granted permissions and session information. Depending on the session Shopify supplies, this can include a merchant or staff member’s user ID, name, email address, language and account role.
  • Menu content and settings: menu names, drafts, published versions, revision history, links, text, translations, images or image references, selected products and collections, layout settings, country and language visibility rules, and custom Liquid code that you save.
  • Store resources: product and collection details, pages, blog content, store languages and market settings accessed to help you build menus and display localized storefront content. Published menu snapshots are also stored in Shopify so they can be selected and displayed through the theme.
  • Storefront requests and status: menu and version identifiers, theme identifier, theme role and the latest time a published menu was detected on the live theme. Menu requests also use language, country and desktop or mobile context to display appropriate content. These status records are not individual visitor profiles or menu click histories.
  • Technical and support information: information you send when requesting support. Hosting services may also process IP addresses, request details, browser information and operational logs when serving and protecting the app.

The app does not request access to Shopify customer or order records. Its current implementation does not include advertising trackers or menu click analytics. Shopify may include customer-related parameters in app-proxy requests or privacy webhook notifications; the app does not use these to build customer profiles or store them as customer records.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate installations, save and restore menu content, publish menus, retrieve selected store resources, apply translations and market rules, show live-menu status, respond to support requests, maintain security and comply with applicable obligations. Published navigation content is intended to be visible to storefront visitors; do not include private information or credentials in menu content or custom code.

Cookies and browser storage

Shopify and the app’s authentication framework may use cookies or similar mechanisms to manage merchant sign-in. On the storefront, MenuPilot uses browser session storage to remember that a particular menu version and theme have already reported their live status. This limits repeat reports for approximately five minutes; it does not create a persistent visitor identifier. The app does not set advertising cookies.

Storefront images, links, fonts or custom content chosen by a merchant may contact other services. Those services can receive technical information when a visitor loads that content or follows a link. The merchant’s privacy policy and the relevant provider’s policy also apply.

Service providers and sharing

MenuPilot uses Shopify for app installation, authentication, store resources and storefront integration, and Google Cloud and Firebase for hosting, application processing and database storage. We disclose information as needed to operate these features, respond to your requests, meet legal obligations or protect the service. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

See the Shopify Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy for information about these providers. The editor’s “Copy AI prompt” feature copies instructions to your clipboard; it does not automatically send your menu content to an AI provider. If you paste content into an external AI service, that service’s terms and privacy policy apply.

Storage and international processing

Production application data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, and the application runs on Google Cloud Run with Firebase Hosting. The application service is configured in the United States. Information may be processed in other countries where Shopify, Google or their service providers operate. Applicable protections and transfer requirements depend on the information and jurisdictions involved.

Retention, uninstalling and deletion

Menu drafts, settings and published-version history are retained to provide the service while the app is installed. When Shopify sends an uninstall notification, the app disables its menus, marks the installation inactive and deletes its stored Shopify sessions. Uninstalling does not immediately erase all saved menu data.

When Shopify subsequently sends a shop-redaction notification, the app deletes its stored installation, menus, menu-version history and remaining sessions for that store. Provider logs, backups and support correspondence can have separate retention periods based on operational needs and applicable requirements. You can contact us to request deletion or ask about retained information.

The app acknowledges Shopify customer data-access and deletion notifications without creating customer records from their contents, because its application database does not store Shopify customer records.

Security

The app uses authenticated Shopify sessions, store-scoped access checks, server-side credential storage and verification of Shopify webhook and app-proxy requests. Administrative access to application data is restricted. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit or remove menu content through the app and uninstall the app through Shopify. To request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or raise a privacy concern, email arif872006@gmail.com. Please identify your store so we can locate the relevant records. We may need to verify that you are authorized to make the request.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights, such as restriction, objection, portability or the right to complain to a data protection authority. Storefront customers should contact the merchant for requests concerning purchases or information held by that merchant.

Children

MenuPilot’s administrative service is intended for merchants and their authorized staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been provided to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
